Acme Staff Handbook

Welcome to Acme. This handbook collects the rules and benefits every employee should
know. It is organized by topic; if you cannot find an answer here, ask the People team
rather than guessing.

Vacation is earned at the rate of two days per month, for a total of twenty-four days
per year. Vacation does not carry over to the next calendar year, so plan to use your
days before December 31. Requests are approved on a first-come, first-served basis within
each team, and no more than ten employees may be on vacation from the same team at once.

Sick leave is separate from vacation and does not reduce your vacation balance. You may
take up to ten paid sick days per year. If you are ill for more than three consecutive
days, you must provide a doctor's note on your return.

The office observes nine public holidays each year. The list is published every January
in the internal calendar. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday
is given instead.

The standard work week is forty hours, Monday through Friday. Core hours are ten in the
morning to four in the afternoon, during which every employee must be available. Outside
core hours, you may arrange your schedule with your manager.

Remote work is allowed for up to two days per week with manager approval. Fully remote
positions require a written arrangement signed by both the employee and the head of
People. Internet reimbursement is capped at fifty dollars per month for remote workers.

New employees receive a laptop and a second monitor within their first week. The standard
laptop is the ThinkPad T14s. You may request a different model, but the request must be
approved by IT and can delay delivery by up to three weeks.

Software purchases under two hundred dollars do not need approval and are reimbursed the
following pay cycle. Purchases above two hundred dollars must be approved by your manager
before you buy. Cloud services with monthly recurring costs always require approval,
regardless of amount.

The travel policy requires all flights to be booked at least fourteen days in advance to
qualify for the best fare. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Business
class is permitted for flights longer than six hours with a manager's pre-approval.

Accommodation during travel is reimbursed up to two hundred and fifty dollars per night.
Meals are reimbursed up to sixty dollars per day. You must submit receipts within thirty
days of returning from a trip, or the expenses will not be paid.

The annual learning budget is one thousand dollars per employee. It can be spent on
courses, books, or conference tickets, but not on hardware. Unused budget does not roll
over to the next year.

Each employee receives a health insurance plan chosen from three options during the open
enrollment period in November. Dental and vision coverage are included in every plan at
no extra cost. Dependents can be added at any time during the year by contacting People.

The 401k plan matches fifty percent of your contribution up to six percent of your salary.
Contributions vest immediately. You can change your contribution percentage at any time,
and the change takes effect on the first of the following month.

Parental leave is twelve weeks of fully paid leave for the birth or adoption of a child.
It must be taken within the first year of the child's arrival. You may split it into two
blocks, but each block must be at least two weeks long.

Bereavement leave is five paid days for the loss of an immediate family member. Immediate
family includes spouse, parents, children, and siblings. Additional unpaid time can be
arranged with your manager.

The office is open from eight in the morning until six in the evening, Monday to Friday.
Badges work twenty-four hours a day, but access to the server room is limited to the
infrastructure team. Visitors must sign in at the front desk and wear a visitor badge.

Parking is available in the building garage for employees who opt in at a cost of sixty
dollars per month. The garage has four hundred spaces. Bicycle storage is free and located
on level one near the elevator.

The kitchen is stocked with free coffee, tea, and fruit. Employees bring their own lunch
or order from the cafeteria on the second floor. The cafeteria serves lunch from noon to
two and dinner is not provided.

The code of conduct requires all employees to treat one another with respect. Harassment
of any kind is prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination. Reports can be made confidentially to the People team or through the
anonymous reporting line.

Security requires that every laptop be encrypted and locked when unattended. Passwords
must be changed every ninety days and must not be reused. Two-factor authentication is
mandatory for all company accounts.

Expense reports are submitted through the finance portal and are paid on the fifteenth
and last day of each month. Reports must be approved by your manager first. Rejected
reports show the reason in the portal and can be resubmitted after correction.

The performance review cycle runs twice a year, in January and July. Each review includes
a self-assessment, a peer review, and a meeting with your manager. Promotions are
considered during the July cycle only.

The annual salary review is tied to the July performance cycle. Merit increases are
budgeted at four percent on average, but individual raises depend on performance rating
and market benchmarks. Off-cycle raises require approval from the compensation committee.

Internal transfers require you to have been in your current role for at least twelve
months. The process starts with a conversation with your manager, followed by an
application in the careers portal. Transfers become effective on the first of the month
after approval.

The resignation notice period is four weeks. Notice is given in writing to your manager
and the People team. Unused vacation days are paid out on your final paycheck.

Alumni are welcome to rejoin within two years of departure without repeating the full
interview process, provided they left in good standing. Rehired employees retain their
original hire date for tenure calculations.
